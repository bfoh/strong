# Strong Pavilion — scene and shot specification

This is conceptual brand architecture, never a listing or a representation of an existing Strong Properties property. The final visual identity is contemporary tropical architecture: a generous travertine/limestone pavilion with sculptural columns, floor-to-ceiling glass, reflecting pool, planted courtyards, subtle bronze edges and furniture. Keep one fixed model, lighting rig and material set throughout both masters.

Use metres. The source `.blend` must be aligned to the shot: Three/world coordinates use Y up; the render script maps them to Blender `(x, -z, y)`. Architectural centre sits near `(0, 1.5, -3)` in the shot coordinates. Covered passage runs alongside the west wall, approximately x=-6.5 and z=-8 to -1; pool lies in the courtyard near z=6. Final model geometry must be reviewed against the entire path, not only the five stills. Adjust shot coordinates for a reviewed model rather than blindly importing unrelated geometry.

Required visual finish: model bevels and construction joints at real scale; mapped high-resolution stone albedo/roughness/normal/displacement; physically plausible glass thickness and IOR; actual pool reflections/refraction; varied botanically plausible tropical vegetation; restrained furnishing; contact shadows and global illumination. One soft tropical afternoon sun and an appropriately licensed HDRI stay fixed. Review exposure, white balance and reflections. Avoid overexposed stone or black interiors. No frame-by-frame AI image generation, geometry morphing or moving landscape.

Cycles, AgX, initial 256 samples with denoising. Start with 10 seconds at 24fps: 240 integer frames, source PNG 16-bit RGB. Desktop master 1920×1080, mobile master 1080×1920 with independently reviewed vertical framing. 32mm lens starting point; no animated focal length, depth-of-field hunting or motion blur in the scroll masters. Final frames must remain sharp at a stationary scroll position. Optional promotional video may be a separate export.

Initial web deliveries: desktop 1280×720, mobile 720×1280 WebP quality80. These are starting values; final scene must be benchmarked and visually inspected. Posters use mid-shot frames at quality87. Canvas uses cover scaling and DPR≤1.5. Review portrait crop at every stage, not merely a desktop crop. The player starts canvas/frame loading only after at least1% of the cinematic viewport is visible. It caches at most12 desktop/8 mobile frames and uses 32MiB/16MiB cached RGBA budgets with two in-flight fetch/decode jobs; transient decode and canvas backing stores are additional bounded allocations.

Asset sources must include ownership or a suitable license for rendered commercial web use. [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) provides CC0 HDRIs, textures and models; it does not establish a license for an unrelated residence model. No final architectural model has been obtained. Record every scene, texture, HDRI and plant asset in the license manifest, including original source and restrictions. The external source package should pack textures and contain no private property documents.

Full rendering is gated on five representative proof frames and a continuous camera playblast, desktop and mobile. Review texture scale, material quality, pool reflections, passage collisions, building consistency, portrait framing and believable speed. Copy approval templates only after review; include the exact source-scene SHA-256. The render script rejects a full render without matching approval and complete asset licenses.
